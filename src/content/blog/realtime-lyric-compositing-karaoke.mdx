Karaoke lives or dies on timing. Lyrics have to hit the beat, highlight the
current word, and sit legibly over whatever the camera is pointed at —
all while the singer watches themselves live.

## Words on the beat

Timed lyric data drives a Smelter scene layered over the camera input. Each
word animates in on cue, the active syllable glows, and the whole caption
block stays readable against a busy background thanks to a compositing pass
that adapts contrast per frame.

It composites on-device and streams out, so a living-room duet looks like a
produced music video.
