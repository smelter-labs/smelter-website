A 50-person call is brutal on client devices if every participant decodes
every other stream. We flipped it: Smelter composites the grid once,
server-side, and relays a single stream out to everyone.

## Active-speaker layout

The layout is driven by audio levels. The active speaker gets the hero tile,
the rest tile around it, and the whole thing re-flows with a spring
transition when the floor changes hands — all as a Smelter scene.

Clients decode one stream instead of fifty. Laptop fans stopped spinning up,
and the call held steady well past the point our old mesh setup used to fall
over.
