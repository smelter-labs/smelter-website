Personalized overlays at scale usually mean one encoder per viewer — and a
cloud bill that grows linearly with your audience. We took a different route.

By compositing overlays server-side in Smelter and sharing the underlying
encode across cohorts, we drove 10,000 concurrent branded streams from a
fraction of the hardware a naive per-stream setup would need.

## The trick

Overlays that only differ in data — a scoreboard, a viewer's handle, a
localized lower-third — don't each need their own encoder. Smelter's scene
graph lets us swap just the changing layers while reusing the heavy lifting.

The result is personalization that reads as bespoke to every viewer, billed
like a single broadcast.
