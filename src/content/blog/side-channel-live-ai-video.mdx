Smelter's side channel hands already-decoded frames — video or audio —
straight to an external process. No re-decoding, no second pipeline. Episode
four puts it to work three different ways at once.

## Three uses, one stream

**Captions, fully local.** Whisper does speech-to-text on the machine, and the
captions are scheduled so they land exactly on the speech rather than trailing
it.

**Repainted cars.** Car detection runs on bird's-eye drone footage and feeds a
WGSL shader that repaints every car live.

**Ghost sprites.** A swarm of sprites chases people around the frame, driven by
YOLO detections at 60 fps.

All three composite together and go out over WebRTC as one live Smelter stream.

Watch the walkthrough above, then try it yourself in the
[live demo](https://workshop.smelter.dev) or read the source in
[smelter-labs/smelter-editor](https://github.com/smelter-labs/smelter-editor).
