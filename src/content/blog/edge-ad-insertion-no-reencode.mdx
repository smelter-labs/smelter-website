Server-side ad insertion usually means decoding, splicing, and re-encoding the
whole feed at every edge node — expensive, and a quality hit each time. We
kept the main encode untouched and composited breaks in at the edge instead.

## Frame-accurate, region-aware

Smelter runs at each edge, holds the program feed, and composites the right ad
creative for the region at the marked break — frame-accurate, with a clean
transition in and out. The primary encode is never round-tripped.

Fill rates went up because the breaks look native, and the CDN bill went down
because we stopped re-encoding the same stream a dozen times over.
