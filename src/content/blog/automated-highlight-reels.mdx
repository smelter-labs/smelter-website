By the time a match ends, the highlights should already be posted. We wired a
detection model into a Smelter pipeline so clips are cut, captioned, and
branded in the same pass the live show runs in.

## Live in, reels out

The live feed is the input. When the model flags a moment, Smelter renders a
vertical, captioned, watermarked cut from the buffered frames and hands it off
to publishing — no separate editing pass, no re-encode of the source.

The social team went from "we'll have clips tomorrow" to clips landing before
the next play starts.
