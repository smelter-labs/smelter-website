We wanted OBS, but as a link you could send someone. No download, no GPU
driver roulette, no "which version are you on?" support threads. Smelter's
web renderer turned out to be exactly the primitive we needed.

## Scenes, sources, transitions

The whole mixer is a React app. Scenes are component trees, sources are
inputs, transitions are animated props. State lives where the rest of the app
state lives, so undo/redo and collaborative editing came almost for free.

Everything composites on the client and goes out over WHIP. For most of our
users, the first time they realize there's no native app is when they try to
find the window in their dock and there isn't one.
